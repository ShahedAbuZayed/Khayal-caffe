OUR STORY

It started with a little dream.

Khayal Café was born from a simple idea: create a place where people could slow down, take a breath, and enjoy the little things.

We wanted to create more than just another coffee shop. We imagined a space filled with soft colors, beautiful flavors, cozy corners, and drinks that make you want to take a picture before the first sip.

From our signature matcha to our creamy iced lattes, every drink is prepared with care and a little bit of imagination.
